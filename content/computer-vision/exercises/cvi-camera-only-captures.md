## Question

A doorbell camera records video to a memory card all day. Nobody's program looks at the video. Is this computer vision?

## Options

- [x] No: it only captures images. Computer vision is the processing that gets information out of them.
  Right: a camera is only step 1. It becomes computer vision when a program, for example, notices that a person is at the door.
- [ ] Yes: anything with a camera is computer vision.
  A camera only records light. That is the most common misunderstanding about computer vision.
- [ ] Yes, because the video is stored as numbers.
  Every digital image is stored as numbers. Computer vision is what a program **does** with those numbers.
- [ ] No, because computer vision only works on single photos, not videos.
  Computer vision works on both images and videos (many images in a row).

## Explanation

Computer vision is about **extracting useful information** from images and videos:

**Image → computer vision system → understanding or a decision**

Recording alone has no understanding step and no decision.
